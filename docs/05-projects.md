# 05 — Projects & Open Source

> **Purpose:** Show that you build in public, collaborate across organizations, and care about engineering craft beyond your day job. For a Lead, OSS is evidence of **influence without authority**, code-review culture, and communication in writing.
>
> **Distinction from case studies:** Case studies = deep, narrative, business-led. Projects = lighter-weight, link-out, artifact-led (repo, demo, package).

- OSS gets its own homepage band, above side projects.

- Avoid a raw "GitHub contribution graph" as the centerpiece — it signals activity, not impact.
